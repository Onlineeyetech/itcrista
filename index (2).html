How long does a typical project take?+
Timelines vary by scope. A standard website takes 3–6 weeks; Shopify stores 2–4 weeks; custom web apps 8–16 weeks. After our discovery call, we provide a detailed timeline with clear milestones before work begins.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch?+
Yes — all projects include a 30-day post-launch support window at no extra cost. We also offer flexible monthly retainer plans for ongoing maintenance, updates, SEO, and feature additions.
Which technologies and platforms do you use?+
We're technology-agnostic and choose the best stack for your project. Our team works with React, Next.js, Node.js, Laravel, Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, AWS, and more.
What industries do you work with?+
We've worked across real estate, e-commerce, healthcare, legal, SaaS, education, hospitality, finance, and more. Our process is adaptable and domain expertise from similar industries accelerates every project.
What does your pricing look like?+
We offer fixed-price project engagements and monthly retainer models. Basic websites start from $2,500; Shopify stores from $1,800; custom apps from $8,000. Book a free consultation for a precise quote.
