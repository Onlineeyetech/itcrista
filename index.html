FAQ

Questions We
Get Asked Often

How long does a typical project take? +

Most projects are completed between 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. A simple website can be delivered in 10–14 days, while a full ecommerce build typically takes 3–5 weeks. We share a detailed timeline during our discovery call.

Do you offer white-label development for agencies? +

Yes! We are a trusted white-label development partner for agencies worldwide. We operate under complete confidentiality — your clients will never know we're involved. We sign NDAs and work within your processes.

What platforms do you specialize in? +

We specialize in Shopify, Shopify Plus, WordPress, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom Next.js / React applications. We're platform-agnostic and will always recommend the best fit for your business.

Will my website be SEO-friendly and fast? +

Absolutely. Every project ships with full technical SEO setup, schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, and we target 90+ scores on Google PageSpeed. Average load time across our projects is under 1 second.

What happens after the website is launched? +

We offer post-launch support ranging from 30 days (Starter) to 12-month retainer plans (Enterprise). Our care plans include security monitoring, backups, updates, bug fixes, and performance tuning.

How do you handle project communication? +

Every client gets a dedicated project manager. We communicate via Slack or WhatsApp with daily/weekly updates and share a Notion board for full project transparency. You're always in the loop.
